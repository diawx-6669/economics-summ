Can you save the island?

How to play

  1. You are the council of Solara. 40 people live here, and resources are scarce.
  2. Jobs: give every worker a job. A new fisher means one less farmer. That is opportunity cost.
  3. Rules, Build and Market: protect fish and forest, build tanks and homes, trade at the pier.
  4. Council: Pedro, Dana, Jonas, Mei and Asel advise you every year. Press Fix to follow their advice.
  5. Press End year. Survive 10 years with food, water, space and happy people.

Watch the island change, read what villagers say in the chat, and point at the map to explore.
