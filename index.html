How we produce

Land

Fertile land, the pond, the fishing area and the jungle. Only Brooklyn can be built on, so land is scarce.

Labour

36 working adults and their skills, from Bek the fisher to Asel the doctor.

Capital

Our first tools came from the shipwreck. Next we build ovens, fences, water tanks and boats.

What do we produce?

Food first: bread, fish, mutton and watermelons. Then water, tools and repairs. Cookies and cake only if we have extra.

How?

People run the farms, fishing and the bakery themselves, but follow the Charter: fishing limits, rain tanks and crop rotation.

For whom?

Basic needs go to everyone equally (Article 1). Extra things you get by trading (Article 22).

Chapter 13

The problem and our plan

Our problem card is Population and Consumption: we use the island faster than it can grow back.

How it happens

More people move into Brooklyn
They need more food and water
Fish and trees cannot grow back in time
Our kids get an empty island
Rule 1 unlocked! · Article 13
Fishing only 3 days a week
Fish have time to grow back
Rule 2 unlocked! · Article 15
Cut 1 tree, plant 2
Wood never runs out
Rule 3 unlocked! · Article 18
Build with bamboo first
It grows back much faster than trees
Rule 4 unlocked! · Article 17
Rain tank on every roof
We do not depend on one pond
Rule 5 unlocked! · Article 16
Maikuduk is protected
Only cocoa and bamboo can be taken
Rule 6 unlocked! · Article 23
Build up, not out
New homes get a 2nd floor, no new land needed

Is the plan worth it?

+ Fish and trees grow back

+ Water for everyone, even in dry weeks

+ The jungle stays for our kids

+ Nobody goes hungry

− People can fish less

− Water tanks need wood and work

− Brooklyn stays crowded for now

− Someone has to check the rules

Trade-off: use a bit less today, so there is enough tomorrow.
