# Solara: an island economy

Economics summative by **Team Common Ground**. Case: Population and Consumption.

Solara is an island we built in Minecraft. It has a mixed economy and two districts: Brooklyn, where people live, and Maikuduk, the protected jungle.

- **Website:** 40 residents, the 26-article Solara Charter, the story of the island and a built-in simulator. Open `index.html`.
- **Game:** *Solara: Island Council*. Run the island for 10 years, share out jobs, pass laws and keep 40 people fed. Open `game/`.
- **Presentation:** `presentation.pdf`, 15 slides.
- **Simulator:** a quick population and consumption model. Open `sim/`.

The numbers in the game and the simulator are a simple model, made up to show the economic ideas: scarcity, opportunity cost, basic needs and future generations.
